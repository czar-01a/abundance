Language models learned from the internet. Robots need data that doesn’t exist yet — so we go out and collect it.

What we capture

Every modality your model learns from.

From first-person video to full teleoperation trajectories, captured by trained contributors in real environments.

Egocentric video

First-person footage of people doing real tasks, with synchronized depth and hand-pose tracking.

Teleoperation demonstrations

Expert-driven robot trajectories with joint states, actions, and camera streams for imitation learning.

Motion capture

Full-body and dexterous hand motion for humanoid control, retargeting, and whole-body policies.

Multimodal sensor data

RGB-D (color and depth), LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging), IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit), audio, and tactile streams — time-aligned.

How it works

From spec to training-ready in three steps.

01

Specify

Tell us the task, embodiment, environments, and volume. We scope a collection plan with you.

02

Capture

Our contributor network records in real-world settings, with calibrated hardware and consistent protocols.

03

Deliver

Annotated, quality-checked episodes arrive in the format your training stack already reads.

Built for

Teams teaching machines to act.

Humanoid robotics

Whole-body and bimanual data for general-purpose humanoids working in homes, warehouses, and factories.

Dexterous manipulation

Fine-grained grasping, tool use, and contact-rich tasks with hand pose and tactile signals.

Robot foundation models

Diverse, large-scale episodes across tasks and environments for VLA (Vision-Language-Action) pretraining.

Evaluation & benchmarking

Held-out, real-world test sets to measure how policies generalize before they ship.

Consent by design

Every contributor and location opts in, with clear licensing for commercial model training.

Privacy protected

Faces and PII (Personally Identifiable Information) are blurred or removed before anything leaves our pipeline.

Quality you can audit

Multi-stage QA (Quality Assurance) with per-episode metadata, so you know exactly what you are training on.
